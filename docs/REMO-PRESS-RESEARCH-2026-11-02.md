# REMO press research

Research date: 2026-11-02 (Asia/Seoul). Found through Naver News search; each article below was opened and its body read. Search snippets alone were not treated as evidence. This research does not change or publish the landing page.

## Sources and usable claims

1. **ZDNet Korea, 2026-10-06** — “충남콘진원, AI페스타서 영상 제작·헬스케어 AI 기술 조명…스타트업 시장 진출 지원”
   - https://zdnet.co.kr/view/?no=20261006172439
   - Reports REMO exhibiting FINEFIT AI Mirror and FINEFIT SIMPro at AI Festa 26, held October 6–8 at COEX, Seoul.
   - Describes ordinary-camera human body and motion analysis; the reporter describes the actual exhibition booth.
   - Explicitly describes Physical AI learning/validation data applications and muscle simulation as research. This does not establish a ready-to-buy humanoid training dataset, a dataset volume, or commercial supply terms.
2. **Daily Economy, 2026-10-23** — “리모x최윤정, 국민건강보험공단 본부서 ‘파인핏 AI 미러’ 연계 직장인 건강관리 특강”
   - https://www.kdpress.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=209483
   - Reports a posture-analysis and exercise education session on October 20 at NHIS headquarters in Wonju, using FINEFIT AI Mirror already introduced there.
   - Supports a specific workplace-wellness deployment/use case. Does not establish procurement value, medical efficacy, endorsement, or permission to use NHIS branding.
3. **Electronic Times, 2025-12-26** — “60회 '하이테크어워드' 개최…AI·빅데이터·보안·오픈소스 등 9개 부문 大賞 시상”
   - https://www.etnews.com/20251226000189
   - Names REMO CEO Bae Eun-kyung as recipient of the AI Healthcare category grand prize, citing development of a Motion LLM system for 3D analysis of body imbalance, posture and gait.
   - Attribute the prize to its recipient and category. Do not confuse it with the separate Physical AI category prize awarded to another company.
4. **Gonggam Newspaper, 2024-04-24** — “리모, FIBO GLOBAL FITNESS 2024에서 '파인핏' 출품”
   - https://www.gokorea.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=752970
   - Reports FINEFIT exhibited at FIBO 2024, with approximately 30 sales/API discussions with European and Middle Eastern buyers and developers.
   - Discussions are not signed contracts or export revenue. This older feature description should not override current product specifications.
5. **Gukje News, 2026-10-26** — “충남콘텐츠진흥원, AI Festa 26서 2개사 상담 25건…2억3,760만원 성과”
   - https://www.gukjenews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=3733298
   - Body attributes 15 business discussions totaling KRW 57.6 million to REMO; 25 discussions/KRW 237.6 million are combined totals for two companies.
   - Headline/subheading and body contain inconsistent aggregate amounts. Do not use the aggregate as REMO's result. All discussion amounts are pipeline, not revenue; a concise attendance claim is preferable for the landing page.
6. **Daily Economy, 2026-01-05, updated 2026-01-09** — “리모, ‘하이테크어워드’ AI 헬스케어 대상...3D체형·보행·모션 분석 LLM 개발”
   - https://www.kdpress.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=201916
   - Corroborates the award and describes FINEFIT AI Mirror as a launched home/office product.
   - Article publication is January 2026; the award event is December 2025, as established by the contemporary Electronic Times report and official company history.

## Recommended use in the landing page

Keep proof compact: explain REMO's camera-based motion AI, show the NHIS workplace use case, cite the 2025 award precisely, and show AI Festa 2026 plus FIBO 2024 as exhibition experience. Link each claim to its source. Keep the three partnership tracks and Shenzhen meeting CTA prominent.

These sources provide existing-product credibility; they do not prove Korean humanoid distribution rights, a formal BOAM–REMO partnership, or immediate availability of humanoid training datasets. Do not present them as such.

## Still unverified

- Images in news articles are not automatically licensed for reuse. Link the articles; obtain images from authorized company materials or an explicit license before copying.
- CHTF 2026 attendance, booth, travel dates and meetings are not independently established by these articles.
